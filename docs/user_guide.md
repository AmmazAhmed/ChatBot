# Inquisitor Chatbot - User Guide

## 1. Getting Started

### Opening the Chat
1. Click the chat bubble icon in the bottom right corner
2. The chat window will slide up

### Sending a Message
1. Type your question in the input field
2. Press Enter or click the Send button
3. Wait for the assistant to respond

## 2. What You Can Ask

### Platform Information
- "What is the Inquisitors Society?"
- "How do I become a member?"

### Courses & Learning
- "How do I enroll in a course?"
- "How do I track my progress?"
- "How do I get a certificate?"

### Events
- "How do I register for an event?"
- "What events are coming up?"
- "How do I volunteer?"

### Internships
- "How do I apply for an internship?"
- "How do I post an internship?"

### Career Development
- "How do I create a portfolio?"
- "How do I use the resume builder?"

### Account Management
- "How do I create an account?"
- "How do I reset my password?"

## 3. Tips for Best Results
- Be specific in your questions
- Use keywords like "register", "enroll", "apply"
- If unsure, use the suggested questions
- For complex issues, contact human support

## 4. Getting Support
- The chatbot will offer follow-up questions
- Click "Contact Support" in the chat
- Email: support@inquisitors.edu
